# Glossary

This glossary explains words that show up in more than one screen.

## Approval
When a person says yes to let a plan or action move forward.

## Pending
Waiting for something to happen.

## Claim
When the app says it will handle a task next.

## Retry
Trying again after something failed or did not finish.

## Workflow
A job made of several steps that happen in order.

## DAG
A way to describe a workflow as a chain of steps connected together.

## Node
One step in a workflow.

## Proposal
A plan the app has made and is showing for review.

## Scope
The area a setting or rule applies to, such as global, project, or agent.

## Provider
A service or model the app can use to do AI work.

## Fallback
A backup choice the app tries if the first one does not work.

## Token
A small piece of text used when the app talks to an AI model.

## Quota
A limit on how much the app can use a service or resource.

## Cron
A short way to write a repeating schedule.

## Vault
The app’s main local storage for records and state.

## Backup
A saved copy of the vault at a moment in time.

## Restore
Putting an old copy back into place, possibly replacing current data.

## Memory
The part of the app that stores information it may need later.

## Vector
A way of storing information so it can be searched or compared later.

## Watcher
The part of the app that notices things and suggests next steps.

## Consent
Permission given before something connects or happens.

## Sync peer
Another device or node the app may connect with.

## MCP
A connection standard for extra tools the AI may use.

## Terminal
A command-line window you can open for a project.

## Simple mode
The app view that uses plain friendly words.

## Developer mode
The app view that shows more technical words and details.

## Status
A short update about what is happening with something right now.

## Commit
Saving settings so the app can use them.
